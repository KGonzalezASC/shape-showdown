# Privacy Policy for Shape Showdown

**Last Updated:** August 22, 2026  
**Effective Date:** August 22, 2026  

Shape Showdown ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") is an independent multiplayer falling-block puzzle combat game. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Game processes, how that data is used, and our strict limits on data collection and storage.

We believe in minimal, transparent data handling. We do not sell user data, track you across the web, build advertising profiles, or monetize your personal information.

---

## 1. Information We Process

### A. Discord Profile Information (In-Game Display Only)
When you launch Shape Showdown through Discord as an Activity, the Game uses the Discord Embedded App SDK to retrieve your public Discord profile details:
- **Discord User ID**
- **Username / Display Name**
- **Avatar URL**

**Purpose and Scope:** This data is used solely to render your player tag and avatar on your game field during an active match session and to identify your connection to the server. We do not use this data to track your activity over time, build behavioural profiles, or share with external entities.

### B. Anonymous Diagnostics and Gameplay Telemetry
To ensure server stability, optimize netcode, and fix synchronization bugs, our servers process technical operational data:
- Match diagnostics (packet latency, tick timing, delta compression rates, simulation rollbacks).
- Game state events (line clears, powerup activations, match completion status).
- Error logs and server performance metrics.

**Anonymous Handling:** All gameplay telemetry and performance diagnostics are collected anonymously and aggregated. They are not linked to your personal identity or persistent user profiles.

### C. No IP Address Logging
We do not record, store, or log player IP addresses in our database. Network connections to our WebSocket game servers use ephemeral socket handles that terminate when you leave the match.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

To be completely clear about our boundaries, Shape Showdown does **not**:
- Collect or store payment information, credit cards, or billing addresses.
- Use tracking cookies, marketing beacons, or browser fingerprinting scripts.
- Integrate third-party advertising networks or data brokers.
- Access your private Discord messages, friend lists, or server memberships.
- Sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information under any circumstances.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- **Ephemeral Match Data:** Active game session data resides in server memory during a match and is purged when the match ends.
- **Analytics Retention:** Aggregated, non-personal performance metrics (such as server bandwidth usage and crash reports) are retained strictly for infrastructure maintenance and debugging.
- **Guest Sessions:** Standalone web guest sessions generate temporary random identifiers that carry no personal data and expire automatically.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services and Platforms

### Discord
When accessing the Game within Discord, your use of the Discord client is governed by the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). Discord independently handles account management, platform telemetry, and voice/chat data according to its own terms.

### Hosting and Infrastructure
Our game servers and static web assets are hosted on infrastructure providers (such as Railway and Cloudflare). These providers process network traffic according to standard security and routing protocols to deliver the Service.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Data Inquiries

Because we do not maintain persistent user accounts or track users across sessions, we hold no stored personal records to query, export, or modify. If you have questions about our data practices or wish to submit an inquiry, you can contact the developer directly.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Shape Showdown complies with applicable children's privacy regulations (including COPPA). The Game requires users to meet Discord's minimum age requirements (13 years or older, or the applicable legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical adjustments or new game features. Any changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued access to the Game after updates confirms your acceptance of the revised policy.

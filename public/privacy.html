Privacy Policy

Shape Showdown • Effective August 22, 2026

Shape Showdown ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") is an independent multiplayer puzzle combat game. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict data minimization principles. We do not sell data, track players across the web, build marketing profiles, or log IP addresses.

01. Information We Process

A. Discord Profile Details (Active Display Only): When launching the Game via Discord, we receive your Discord User ID, username, and avatar URL via the Discord Embedded App SDK. This information is used strictly to render your player tag and avatar on your game field during an active match. We do not store or use this data to track users across sessions.

B. Anonymous Telemetry & Diagnostics: To ensure server stability, optimize packet bandwidth, and debug synchronization issues, we record anonymous, aggregated telemetry (such as frame timings, packet drop rates, and error traces). These operational logs are not tied to individual player identities.

C. No IP Address Logging: We do not log, record, or store player IP addresses in our database. Network connections use temporary socket identifiers that expire when the match ends.

02. Information We Do Not Collect

To maintain an ethical and clean platform, Shape Showdown does not:

  • Collect or store payment or credit card details.
  • Use tracking cookies, marketing beacons, or fingerprinting scripts.
  • Integrate third-party ad networks or data brokers.
  • Access your private Discord messages, channels, or friend lists.
  • Sell, rent, trade, or monetize personal information.

03. Data Retention & Sessions

Match session state is held in ephemeral server memory only for the duration of the match and is purged immediately upon completion. Standalone web guest sessions generate random temporary IDs that carry no personal identifiers and expire automatically.

04. Third-Party Platforms

Discord: When playing through Discord, your account is subject to the Discord Privacy Policy. Discord independently manages user authentication and voice/chat data.

Hosting: Our game servers and static assets run on infrastructure providers (Railway and Cloudflare) that handle standard network routing under secure protocols.

05. Children's Privacy

Shape Showdown is not directed toward children under 13, in compliance with COPPA and Discord minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly process personal data from children under 13.

06. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical updates. Revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
